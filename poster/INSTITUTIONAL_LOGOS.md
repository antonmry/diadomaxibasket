# Institutional-logo banner

`institutional-logos-source.png` is the supplied master banner and retains its light, mid-tone, and dark variants.

- `institutional-logos.png` is the light 2000 × 320 px strip used at the bottom of the poster.
- `../dist/assets/institutional-logos.png` is the matching website-ready light strip.
- `../dist/assets/institutional-logos-white.png` is the dark-footer treatment used by the website.

To replace or extend the logo set, update the master banner with consistent spacing, then export a 2000 × 320 px light strip and a white-on-transparent 2000 × 320 px strip. Keep the filenames and dimensions above; the poster and website will update without markup or layout changes.
